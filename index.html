Settings

Game speed

2x makes everything on the board happen twice as fast. The music keeps its own pace.

Music

Every room on the board adds its own part to the tune. Group events play a theme of their own.

Testing

Unlocks every character, board space and Compendium entry, secrets too, and gives you 9999 coins. No discovery rewards are paid.

Start over

Wipes your save: the board, coins, unlocks and the Compendium. Tap twice to confirm.

How to play

The board

  • Drag a box from the shelf (under the board) onto an empty space, or tap it to drop it on the first free space.
  • Drag a box on the board to move it, or onto another box to swap them.
  • Dragging a box back onto the shelf costs 10 coins.
  • Dark spaces with a padlock are locked. Tap one to buy it with coins.

Characters

  • Each character lives in their own room. They do things on their own, wander around and visit their neighbors.
  • Neighbors are the boxes touching a side: left, right, above and below. Corners don't count.
  • Where you put each box matters. Who is next to whom, and on which side, decides what happens when they meet. Some things only happen in a certain row or space.
  • Characters can get hurt or even die. A box whose character dies goes back to the shelf.
  • New characters unlock when you do certain things. A locked box on the shelf shows a clue.

The Compendium

  • Everything you see happen is written down in the Compendium. Undiscovered entries show a "?"; hover over or tap one for a hint.
  • Every 5 discoveries pays 10 coins. Finding everything in a character's page gives an artifact, and every 50 discoveries gives a random item.
  • While you drag a box, rooms with a faint blue glow still have entries left to find (secrets not counted).
  • Secrets are hidden until you stumble on them.

Quests

  • The Quests tab lists goals to reach, each with a reward. A quest's reward is paid once, as soon as you reach the goal.

Weather

  • The icon in the top-right corner of the board shows the weather. Tap it to see what the weather does.
  • Every 10 minutes the weather may change. Partly Cloudy does nothing. When it's Sunny, characters visit more. When it's Rainy, they stay home more. A rare Fae Wind makes the rarest events much more likely.

Coins and items

  • Coins come from scenes, sales, customers and discoveries. Nothing earns coins just by waiting.
  • Shop rooms have a small gold coin in the bottom-right corner. Tap it to buy.
  • Items (spell scroll, potion, artifact, ingot) go in the tray above the board. Drag one onto a room while its owner is home. Each character reacts in their own way. If nothing happens, you keep the item.

Events and buildings

  • Every few minutes a group event can happen when characters stand in the right shape, such as three in a row.
  • Buildings like the Well have nobody living in them, but they give their neighbors new things to do.

Saving

  • The game saves by itself in this browser.
